# Roadmap

This roadmap is a statement of intent, not a promise.

## After 0.1.0

- Optional Ecto types for identifier and hostname fields.
- On the next pinned Unicode data version, regenerate conflict keys, review collisions, and migrate application-owned indexes before use.

## Out of scope

- DNS lookup, public-suffix lists, and browser URL parsing.
- Authorization, ownership, and name reservation.
- CONTEXTO and IDNA2008 registration conformance.
